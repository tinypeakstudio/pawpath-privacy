Paw Path – Privacy Policy

Tinypeak Studio · Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Paw Path (Android package com.tinypeakstudio.pawpath), made by Tinypeak Studio ("we"), handles. In short: we have no accounts, no servers and we do not collect personal data ourselves. The game uses Google services for ads, usage statistics and crash reports, which are described below.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (completed levels, coins, outfits, hints, daily streak and settings) is saved only on your device. We cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

No accounts and no personal data collected by us

Paw Path has no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not sell personal data.

Third-party services

The app uses the following Google services. They process some data on our behalf, under Google's own policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising). To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent with Google's consent form before ads are personalised; without consent only non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time under Settings → Privacy choices in the game (where offered), and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. How Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Firebase Analytics (usage statistics). Records anonymous gameplay events such as levels started and completed, hints used and ads shown, together with an app-instance identifier and basic device information. We have turned off advertising-ID collection for Analytics. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Analytics only starts after you agree in the consent form: there, your consent is the legal basis for it. Elsewhere it runs from the start. You can change your analytics consent, as well as your ad consent, any time under Settings → Privacy choices in the game (where offered).
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports). If the app crashes, it sends a crash report with technical details (device model, OS version, app state at the time of the crash) and a random installation identifier, so we can fix bugs. Crash reports are processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the game stable. You can object to this by emailing tinypeakstudio.app@gmail.com.

Analytics and crash data is linked only to these random app-instance and installation identifiers, never to your name or email. You can reset these identifiers by clearing the app's data or reinstalling the app.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy · Firebase privacy information.

Data retention

Data on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Analytics data is kept for 2 months and then deleted automatically. Crash reports are kept for 90 days and then deleted.

Children

Paw Path is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

Data sent to Google's services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Your choices

  • Change ad and analytics consent: Settings → Privacy choices in the game (shown where required by law).
  • Limit ad personalisation or delete your advertising ID: your device's Google / privacy settings.
  • Delete everything stored by the game on your device: uninstall the app.
  • Reset the analytics and crash-report identifiers: clear the app's data or reinstall the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

For questions or requests about your data (for example access or deletion), contact tinypeakstudio.app@gmail.com. Because we don't know your name or email, we can handle a request only where we can identify the data that belongs to you.
